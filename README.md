# Your First AI System

Three agents that learn your business, learn how you sound, and write content that is unmistakably yours. Built for the DQ Shift masterclass at DQuarters, Dubai, November 2026.

It works with Claude, ChatGPT or any other AI assistant. Everything here is plain text files. No code, no GitHub account, nothing to install.

## Why this works

Most AI writing sounds the same because it knows nothing about you. This system fixes that by giving the AI two things before it writes a word: a file about your business, and a file about your voice.

The agents only learn from what you give them. **The input is the system.**

## The three agents

| Agent | You give it | It gives you |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Research Agent** | Your website, social profiles, documents, and answers to its questions | Your **Business File** |
| **2 Tone Agent** | Things you have written yourself | Your **Voice File** |
| **3 Copywriter** | A topic, plus both files | A post, page or email in your voice, then updates to both files from your feedback |

The files are the connection. Each agent hands its file to the next, and your feedback improves both files every time you use it.

## Start in four steps

1. **Download** this folder: click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**, and unzip it.
2. **Open a Project** in Claude or ChatGPT and name it after your business. Step by step guides are in the *setup* folder.
3. **Add the files.** Paste *setup/Project Instructions.md* into the Project's instructions, and add the three files from the *agents* folder to the Project's files.
4. **Start.** Type your website address and answer the Research Agent's questions.

Using another AI assistant, or no Projects? See *setup/Setup for any AI.md*.

## Have this ready

- Your website address
- Links to your company and personal social profiles
- Three to five things you wrote yourself: posts, emails, notes to your team

## What is in this folder

```
agents      the three agents
setup       how to set it up in Claude, ChatGPT or any other AI
guides      good and great input, your folder, the learning loop, adding agents
templates   blank Business File, Voice File and a template for your next agent
```

## You do not build it in a day

You build it, use it, learn and improve. Start with good input today. Add great input this week. Save the updated files back every time an agent gives you one. Then add your next agent. See *guides/Add Your Next Agent.md*.
