# Business Brain

Four agents that learn your business, learn how you sound, write content that is unmistakably yours, and keep getting better every week. Built for the DQ Shift masterclass at DQuarters, Dubai, November 2026.

It works with Claude, ChatGPT or any other AI assistant. Everything here is plain text files. No code, no GitHub account, nothing to install.

## Why this works

Most AI writing sounds the same because it knows nothing about you. Business Brain fixes that by giving the AI two things before it writes a word: a file about your business, and a file about your voice.

The agents only learn from what you give them. **The input is the system.**

## Four agents, four commands

| Type | Agent | You give it | It gives you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **/research** | Research Agent | Your website, documents, and answers to its questions | Your **Business File** |
| **/tone** | Tone Agent | Things you have written yourself | Your **Voice File** |
| **/write** | Copywriter | A topic, plus both files | A post, page or email in your voice, then updates to both files from your feedback |
| **/optimise** | Optimiser | Nothing: it reads everything | The three things that would improve your brain most this week |

Type **/brain** at any time to see what is in place and what to do next.

The files are the connection. Each agent hands its file to the next, your feedback improves both files every time you write, and the Optimiser keeps the whole thing honest.

## Start in three steps

1. **Download.** On this page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**, and unzip it. No GitHub account needed.
2. **Drag in one file.** Open a new chat in Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or any AI assistant and drag in **Business Brain.md**.
3. **Type run brain.** It shows the commands and asks for your website. That is it.

**Want it to remember between chats?** Put **Business Brain.md** in a Project's files instead (Claude and ChatGPT both have Projects), then type **run brain** in any chat inside that Project. Add your Business File and Voice File to the same Project once the agents make them.

**Using the Claude desktop app with your folder connected?** Type: read Business Brain.md and run brain. It reads the whole folder and saves your files straight into it.

**No Projects?** Each time, drag in Business Brain.md plus your Business File and Voice File, and type run brain.

Using Claude Code or the Claude desktop app? Install it as a plugin with `/plugin marketplace add MENAMarketer/BusinessBrain`, then `/plugin install brain@businessbrain`. Details in *Start Here/Setup for Claude.md*.

## Have this ready

- Your website address
- Three to five things you wrote yourself: emails, posts, notes to your team
- Links to your social profiles, if you write your own posts

## This folder is your brain

```
1 Agents        the four agents, and a template for your next one
2 My Business   your Business File, creds deck, brand guidelines
3 My Voice      your Voice File, your writing, transcripts of you talking
4 Content       drafts and published pieces
5 Library       reviews, competitor material, research, past work
Start Here      setup guides, good and great input, the learning loop
Business Brain.md  the whole system in one file: drag this into any AI
skills, tools   the Claude plugin wiring and the build script (you can ignore these)
```

Keep everything in here. It is yours, whichever AI you use.

## You do not build it in a day

The process and the structure work from day one. The detail is yours to add. Start with good input today and add great input this week. Save the updated files back every time an agent gives you one. Run **/optimise** every Friday. Then add your next agent: see *Start Here/Add Your Next Agent.md*.
