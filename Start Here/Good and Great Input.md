# Good and Great Input

Good input gets you started today. Great input makes it yours.

The agents are only as good as what you give them. A better prompt will not fix thin input. A richer Business File and Voice File will.

| Agent | Good: today | Great: this week |
|---|---|---|
| **Research Agent** | Your website. One line on what you sell and who buys | Your company and personal social profiles. Three competitors you lose to. Your creds deck, company profile or brand guidelines. Customer reviews. The questions customers ask most |
| **Tone Agent** | Three to five things you wrote yourself | 10 to 20 pieces of different types. A transcript of you talking. Words you use and words you never use. A piece you hated and why |
| **Copywriter** | The topic. Who it is for. Where it is going | Your view in a sentence. One story or number from your own work. What the reader should do next. Posts you admired |

## The single best thing to add

A transcript of you talking. A podcast, a panel, an interview, or a ten minute voice note about your business, transcribed. Most people write more stiffly than they speak. The transcript shows the agent how you actually sound.

## What good and great look like in a post

**Good input: website only**

> In today's fast paced world, logistics matters more than ever. At Harbour & Co, we deliver seamless, innovative solutions that help businesses thrive. Partner with us and unlock your potential.

**Great input: website, a real story and the Voice File**

> Last Ramadan, a client's 40 containers of dates sat at Jebel Ali for six days.
>
> The paperwork was right. The broker's system was not.
>
> We now check every customs file twice, 48 hours before the ship docks. It costs us a morning. It saved that client AED 120K this year.
>
> Nobody buys freight forwarding for the brochure. They buy it for the call at 2am that says it's sorted.

Harbour & Co is a made up business, used to show the difference.
