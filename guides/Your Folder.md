# Your Folder

Keep everything for your AI system in one folder on your computer. It becomes your system's memory, and it is yours whichever AI you use.

```
My AI System
  1 Agents        the agent files, including any you add later
  2 My Business   Business File, creds deck, brand guidelines, company profile
  3 My Voice      Voice File, writing samples, talk transcripts
  4 Content       drafts and published pieces
  5 Library       reviews, competitor material, research, past work
```

## How to use it

1. **Set it up once.** Make the five folders. Copy the three agent files into *1 Agents*.
2. **Save every file the agents give you** into the right folder, replacing the old version.
3. **Drop in material as you find it.** A good review, a competitor's brochure, a speech you gave. You do not need to sort it perfectly.
4. **Call the agent in to read it.** For example: "I have added our creds deck and 12 customer reviews. Read them and update my Business File."
5. **Keep your best published work** in *4 Content*. Later you can ask the Copywriter to learn from it.

## Keep out

Do not put anything in here you would not want an AI tool to read: passwords, bank details, contracts, other people's personal data. Check your AI provider's data settings if you are unsure.
