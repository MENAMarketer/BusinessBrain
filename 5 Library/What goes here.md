# Library

Drop in anything that holds what your business has learned: customer reviews, competitor brochures, research, past proposals, a speech you gave. You do not need to sort it perfectly.

When you add something, ask the Research Agent: "I have added [what] to my Library. Read it and update my Business File."

Keep out anything you would not want an AI tool to read: passwords, bank details, contracts, other people's personal data.
