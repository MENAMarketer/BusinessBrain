# Business Brain: Your Next Steps

*What to do after the DQ Shift masterclass, step by step*

In the masterclass you built a Business Brain with four agents, a Business File and a Voice File. That was the quick start. This guide takes you from a working system to a rich one, then to building agents of your own. Do it in one sitting of about two hours, or across a week.

The rule that runs through all of it: the agents only learn from what you give them. The more of your real material they read, the more the output sounds like you and knows your business.

## 1. Set up your folder (10 minutes)

1. Find the Business Brain folder you downloaded. If you did not download it, go to github.com/MENAMarketer/BusinessBrain, click the green Code button, then Download ZIP, and unzip it.
2. Rename the folder Business Brain and move it into your Documents folder.
3. Open it. You will see five folders: 1 Agents, 2 My Business, 3 My Voice, 4 Content and 5 Library. Everything you gather goes into these.
4. Save the Business File and Voice File you made in class: Business File into 2 My Business, Voice File into 3 My Voice.

## 2. Build your writing document (30 minutes)

This is the single most valuable thing you can do. It teaches the system how you really sound.

1. Open Word and create a new document. Call it My Writing.
2. Paste in 10 to 20 pieces you wrote yourself. Mix the types: LinkedIn posts, emails, proposals, speeches, notes to your team, even WhatsApp messages.
3. For LinkedIn: open your profile, go to Activity, then Posts, and copy the text of posts you wrote. AI tools cannot open LinkedIn links, so copying is the only way.
4. Put a short heading above each piece saying what it is, for example: Email to a client, March 2026.
5. Only include writing that is yours. If an agency or a colleague writes your posts, leave them out. They will teach the system the wrong voice.
6. Record a 10 minute voice note on your phone, talking about your business as you would to a friend. Transcribe it with your phone, or with Dictate in Word, and paste the transcript in. People talk more naturally than they write, so this is gold.
7. At the end, add two short lists: words and phrases you use a lot, and words you would never use.
8. Save it in 3 My Voice.

## 3. Gather your business documents (30 minutes)

These teach the system what your company is, who it serves and why clients choose it. Word and PDF files both work.

### Into 2 My Business

- Your credentials deck or company profile
- Brand guidelines
- Pitch decks and proposals that won
- Your website's home, about and services page text, pasted into a Word document

### Into 5 Library

- Customer reviews and testimonials
- Case studies
- The questions customers ask most
- Notes on three competitors you lose to, and why

### Keep out

- Contracts, fees and financial accounts
- Client data and anything a client gave you in confidence
- Passwords and personal details

## 4. Make your Business Brain rich (20 minutes)

### In Claude or ChatGPT

1. Create a Project and name it after your business.
2. Add these files to the Project: Business Brain.md, your Business File, your Voice File, My Writing, and your five to ten most important business documents.
3. Start a new chat inside the Project and type: research. I have added my credentials, brand guidelines and reviews. Read them and update my Business File.
4. Answer its questions, then save the updated Business File: replace the old one in your folder and in the Project.
5. Type: tone. I have added My Writing. Read it and update my Voice File.
6. Do its 60 word test honestly, then save the updated Voice File the same way.

### In the Claude desktop app, with your folder connected

1. Connect your Business Brain folder.
2. Type: read Business Brain.md, then read everything in my folder and update my Business File and Voice File.
3. It saves both files straight back into your folder.

When both files are updated, your Business Brain is ready to work properly.

## 5. Start writing (every week)

| Type | What it does |
|---|---|
| start | Checks what is in place and offers a menu of things to write |
| write | Writes anything, in your voice, from your Business File |
| research | Adds new business material to your Business File |
| tone | Adds new writing to your Voice File |
| optimise | Your Friday check: the three things that would help most |
| new agent | Builds a new agent with you, one question at a time |

### Things to ask for

- write a thought leadership article on [topic]. My view is [one sentence].
- write a LinkedIn post about [a recent win or lesson].
- write our about page.
- write an email to prospects about [offer].
- write a company profile for proposals.

### Writing as yourself, and as your company

1. Your Voice File is you. Use it for anything under your own name.
2. For the company, type: tone. Build a second Voice File called Company Voice from my brand guidelines and website.
3. Save Company Voice in 3 My Voice and add it to your Project.
4. Then say which one you want: write as me, or write as the company.

### Every time it writes

1. Tell it what you would change. Be specific: too formal, lead with the story, I never say excited to announce.
2. When it gives you an updated file, save it over the old one. Ten seconds. That is how it learns.

## 6. Every Friday: optimise (10 minutes)

1. Open a chat in your Project and type: optimise.
2. It reads everything and tells you the three things that would help most: questions you never answered, material it has not read, feedback you keep repeating.
3. Do the top one before next Friday.

## 7. Build your next agents

Four agents is a start. The systems we run began with a few and now have dozens. Each one does one job and hands its file to the next.

### Decide what to build by talking it through

1. Open a chat in your Project and type: I run [your business]. Here is a normal week for me: [describe it]. Interview me one question at a time, then tell me which tasks repeat every week and would make good agents.
2. Answer honestly. Talking is faster than planning.
3. Pick one job that repeats every week and follows roughly the same steps. If you cannot describe the steps, an agent cannot follow them.

### Build it

1. Type: new agent. It interviews you and gives you the finished agent as a file.
2. Save the file in 1 Agents and add it to your Project.
3. Test it on a real task this week. Tell it what you would change, and save the updated file.
4. Add one new agent every two weeks. In three months you will have a team.

### Ideas to start from

| Area | Agent | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | Competitor watch | A one page note on what changed this month |
| Marketing | Campaign brief | A brief your agency or team can work from |
| Sales | Meeting follow up | A follow up email and action list from your notes |
| Sales | Pitch builder | A pitch outline from the prospect's website and your Business File |
| Data | Numbers summary | The story in a spreadsheet you export each month |
| Creative | Content calendar | A month of post ideas, each with its angle |
| Leadership | Board update | A one page update in your voice |

### Four rules that keep it working

1. One job per agent. When you keep asking an agent for something outside its job, build a new one.
2. Agents hand files to each other. The files are the memory.
3. Give it great input. The input is the system.
4. You decide. The agents propose, write and check. You approve.

## 8. Let your AI help you build the rest

This workshop was a quick start. From here, use Claude or your own AI to design the whole system with you. Paste this into a chat in your Project:

> Here is my Business Brain. Interview me about my business, my team and my week, one question at a time. Then propose the next five agents I should build, in order, with what each one reads, what it produces, and which agent it hands to.

Build, use, learn, improve. That is how every system we run was built.
