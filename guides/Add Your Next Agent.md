# Add Your Next Agent

Three agents is a start. Our own system began with three and now runs dozens. Each one does one job well, and they hand work to each other through files.

## Before you build one

Pick a job you do every week that follows roughly the same steps. If you cannot describe the steps, the agent cannot follow them.

## Every good agent has five parts

1. **One job.** Not "help with marketing". "Turn my meeting notes into a follow up email."
2. **Good and great input.** What it needs to start, and what makes it excellent.
3. **Questions it asks.** One at a time, the things a sharp new hire would ask.
4. **A file it produces**, in the same shape every time.
5. **A handover.** Where the file is saved, and which agent reads it next.

Use *templates/New Agent Template.md* to write it. Save it in *1 Agents* and add it to your project.

## Ideas for your next agent

| Agent | Reads | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Competitor Watch** | Business File, competitor websites | A one page note on what changed this month |
| **Pitch Builder** | Business File, Voice File, the prospect's website | A pitch outline and the story for each slide |
| **Board Update** | Business File, your notes and numbers | A one page update in your voice |
| **Meeting Follow Up** | Business File, Voice File, your meeting notes | A follow up email and an action list |

## Let the system tell you

When you keep asking an agent for something outside its job, that is the signal to build a new agent, not to stretch the old one. An agent that tries to do everything ends up doing nothing well.

## Ask for help building it

Paste the template into a chat and say: "Interview me, one question at a time, and fill this in for a new agent that [does the job]."
